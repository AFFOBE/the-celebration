Frequently Asked Questions

We are officially associated with Omera Banquet (grand classic palatial ballroom), Omaya Banquet (contemporary luxury banquet with designer lighting), and Kiddtopia (curated kids celebration arena and indoor play paradise). Additionally, we can also execute bespoke event production at selected outdoor estates.

Yes, absolutely. We specialize in sacred spiritual events including Mata Ki Chowki and Jagran. We provide authentic golden temple darbars, akhand jyoti arrangements, fresh floral garlands, gadda/bolster floor seating, certified sound engineers for devotional singing, and complete satvik bhog catering.

Our master culinary team crafts custom gourmet menus covering Traditional Shahi Indian, Mughlai, Awadhi, Live Chaat Street, Continental, Pan-Asian, Italian Live Pasta counters, and opulent royal dessert lounges.
